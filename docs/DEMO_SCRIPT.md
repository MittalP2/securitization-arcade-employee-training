# Study Arcade — five-minute demo script

## Before the demo

Prepare Day 1 with all five lesson slides completed and submit the quiz with one or two intentionally incorrect answers. Leave Coach Debrief unopened so Fireworks can generate it live, and keep the Learning Map closed.

## 0:00–0:30 — Open with the problem

**Show:** The Study Arcade home screen.

**Say:** “Most organizational training disappears into a slide deck: the trainer has to decide what to cover, the employee struggles to see how the concepts connect, and neither gets a useful learning loop. Study Arcade turns onboarding into a guided, reusable learning experience.”

## 0:30–1:00 — Establish the platform

**Show:** Select **Current Topic** and briefly reveal the Learning Library.

**Say:** “Study Arcade is the platform; Securitization Fundamentals is the first topic. The same experience can support company-specific paths such as ABS Suite Sales or Marketing Enablement, grounded in approved internal material.”

**Action:** Close the Learning Library and point to **Take a tour** without running the entire tour.

**Say:** “A guided tour helps a new employee understand the product without a trainer explaining every control.”

## 1:00–1:40 — Show the connected curriculum

**Show:** Open **Learning Map**.

**Say:** “This is both the course index and the learner’s mental model. It shows all 30 core days plus two advanced levels, the learning phases, completed days, and prerequisite connections—so Day 14 never feels disconnected from what the learner saw on Day 3.”

**Action:** Select Day 1 from the map and close it.

## 1:40–2:25 — Demonstrate colleague-led learning

**Show:** The Day 1 lesson and its **Previously → Today → Later** thread. Move through two lesson sections, then select **Present Day 1**.

**Say:** “The trainer gets a clear teaching boundary: what the employee already needs, what to explain today, and what this unlocks later. Present mode supports the live session, while the same material remains with the employee afterward—no separate slide handoff.”

**Action:** Exit presentation mode.

## 2:25–3:05 — Show independent learning activities

**Show:** Flip one flashcard, then open the submitted quiz.

**Say:** “Flashcards support recall, and the quiz gives immediate explanations. There is deliberately no 80% gate: submitting an attempt is what matters. Lesson completion is even simpler—it depends only on completing the lesson slides.”

**Action:** Point to the green Day 1 completion tick and the **Finish lesson** button.

## 3:05–4:05 — Demonstrate the agent

**Show:** Select **Coach Debrief** and let it generate.

**Say:** “This is the agentic part. The Learning Coach receives only the submitted quiz evidence, maps missed answers to approved course concepts, and asks Fireworks AI for a grounded explanation and practical example. It does not invent a new syllabus or score employee performance.”

**Action:** Show the quiz statistics, one missed-question explanation, and select **Show me another example** if time permits.

**Say:** “The employee gets useful reteaching at the moment of need, while the answer remains grounded in the approved lesson context.”

## 4:05–4:35 — Separate curriculum feedback

**Show:** Close Coach Debrief and open **Lesson feedback**.

**Say:** “Curriculum feedback is a different loop. It asks whether the lesson was clear and useful, and what the curriculum developer should improve. It is never passed to the quiz or the Coach.”

**Action:** Close the form without submitting during the demo.

## 4:35–5:00 — Close with the product story

**Show:** The full three-panel workspace.

**Say:** “Study Arcade gives an organization one reusable surface for colleague-led training, employee self-study, connected curriculum, practice, and evidence-based coaching. This demo uses securitization, but the product is designed to turn any approved organizational knowledge into an engaging onboarding journey.”

## If the Coach is slow

Say: “The app keeps the learner workflow safe: if the model cannot produce a grounded result, it stops and records a technical failure for the app owner instead of showing an invented answer.” Then continue directly to Lesson Feedback and the closing statement.
